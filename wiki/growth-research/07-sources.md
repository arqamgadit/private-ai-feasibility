# 7. Sources and method

[Back to wiki home](../README.md) · [Back to the main report](00-main-report.md)

Research compiled **20 November 2026**. This page lists every external source cited in the growth research, grouped by topic, with what each was used for and how far to trust it.

## How the research was done

| Method | What it gave | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| Web search and page fetches | Company pages, rating-agency notes, filings, regulatory documents, press | Two pages could not be fetched directly and were read from search-engine copies (marked below) |
| Free public Semrush overview pages | Monthly visits, organic and paid search visits, top keywords, brand search volume. October 2026 data, US keyword database, pulled 20 November 2026 | Estimates from a panel. No history, no keyword-level spend. The free page was missing for some domains (protonvpn.com, mullvad.net, windscribe.com, aura.com) |
| Free public Similarweb page | Visits, channel mix, country mix for purevpn.com. October 2026 data, pulled 20 November 2026 | Desktop-weighted. Disagrees with Semrush on totals and on country mix |
| Google Play US listings | Ratings, review counts, download bands. Pulled 20 November 2026 | Download bands are coarse |
| Apple iTunes Search API, US storefront | iOS ratings and rating counts. Pulled 20 November 2026 | US only |
| Existing wiki pages | Private AI plan, Venice and "VPN for AI" research | Linked, not repeated |

**Tools that were not available.** The Semrush, Ahrefs, Synapse, SuperMe and conqueror connectors all required authentication that this research did not have. Nothing in this report comes from them. The X connector was available and was not needed.

**Kinds of source.** "Primary" means the organisation's own page, filing or dataset. "Rating agency" notes draw on company data under confidentiality and are the best public source on Nord Security. "Review or affiliate site" means a publication that earns commission on VPN sales or belongs to a VPN group. "Vendor claim" means an unverified statement by a seller about itself. "Secondary" means a site summarising other people's data.
