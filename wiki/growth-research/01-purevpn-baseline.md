# 1. PureVPN baseline

[Back to wiki home](../README.md) · [Back to the main report](00-main-report.md)

Research compiled **20 November 2026**. This page sets out what is publicly known about PureVPN today, and where the report has had to estimate. Every later page builds on the baseline defined here.

## How to read the numbers

- **Sourced** figures carry an inline link to a page that was opened during this research.
- **Claim** means the company said it about itself and nobody independent has confirmed it.
- **Estimate** means our own calculation. Each one states its inputs and a confidence level.
- **Tool data** names the tool, the metric, the country or database, the data month and the date it was pulled. The paid Semrush, Ahrefs and Similarweb interfaces were not available to this research. The figures below come from the free public pages those tools publish.

### Our estimate of paying subscribers

Surfshark is the best yardstick because it has published a recent paying-user number: 4 million, crossed in late February 2026 ([Surfshark executive on LinkedIn, 4 Mar 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/regimantasurbanas_if-you-invest-in-stocks-would-you-consider-activity-7434851400632868864-xOYF)). The table scales that number by four public signals of relative size.

| Signal (all pulled 20 Nov 2026) | PureVPN | Surfshark | Ratio | Implied PureVPN payers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google Play review count, US store | 42.9K ([listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gaditek.purevpnics&hl=en&gl=US)) | 329K ([listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.surfshark.vpnclient.android&hl=en&gl=US)) | 13.0% | 0.52M |
| iOS App Store rating count, US store (iTunes Search API) | 7,960 | 133,856 | 5.9% | 0.24M |
| Website visits, October 2026 (Semrush free overview, all devices, worldwide) | 1.31M ([page](https://www.semrush.com/website/purevpn.com/overview/)) | 10.7M ([page](https://www.semrush.com/website/surfshark.com/overview/)) | 12.2% | 0.49M |
| US searches for the brand name (Semrush free overview, US database, October 2026) | 5,400 for "purevpn" | 110,000 for "surfshark" | 4.9% | 0.20M |

**Estimate: about 0.6 million paying direct subscribers, range 0.3 to 1.2 million. Confidence: low to medium.**

- The four signals cluster between 0.2M and 0.5M.
- We set the centre above that cluster for three reasons. PureVPN sells five-year plans, and long-plan holders review and search less often. The Ivacy migration added subscribers who never installed through a PureVPN store listing. And the same comparison against NordVPN's web traffic gives a higher answer (7.8% of [nordvpn.com's 16.78M visits](https://www.semrush.com/website/nordvpn.com/overview/)).
- The upper bound of 1.2M would need PureVPN to earn far more per visit and per review than Surfshark does. Nothing public supports that.
- The existing wiki assumed about 1 million subscribers ([market research page](../07-market-research.md)). That figure divided an assumed $60M of revenue by $60 a year. The evidence here points lower.

### What customers complain about

- **Trustpilot** shows 4.4 out of 5 from 18,006 reviews, with only 481 reviews in the last 12 months ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/purevpn.com); the page blocked direct fetching, so these figures come from the search-engine copy of the page). The two tools disagree on total visits by about 60%. That is normal for panel-based estimates. Both agree on the shape.

**What the shape says.**

1. **Brand demand is small.** "purevpn" gets 5,400 US searches a month. "nordvpn" gets 301,000, "expressvpn" 135,000 and "surfshark" 110,000 (Semrush free overviews for [nordvpn.com](https://www.semrush.com/website/nordvpn.com/overview/), [expressvpn.com](https://www.semrush.com/website/expressvpn.com/overview/) and [surfshark.com](https://www.semrush.com/website/surfshark.com/overview/), US database, October 2026).
2. **Organic traffic leans on piracy and free sports-stream keywords.** These visitors want free content. They are a weak base for a trust-led brand, and the pages that attract them carry legal and reputational risk.
3. **PureVPN does not buy search traffic.** NordVPN's site received 1.13M paid-search visits in October 2026 and Surfshark's 296K, against PureVPN's 362.
4. **Affiliates matter.** The programme pays 100% of the first payment on monthly plans, 40% on longer plans and 35% of renewals for life, with a 90-day cookie ([PureVPN affiliates](https://www.purevpn.com/affiliates)). The share of orders that comes through affiliates is not public.

## Open gaps

- Paying subscribers, revenue, churn and plan mix are all estimated. PureVPN's own numbers would replace section 2 in one step.
- The share of new orders by channel (affiliate, organic, direct, app store, partner) is unknown.
- The number of active end users served through PureWL partners, and what partners pay per user, is unknown.
- The identity of the current no-logs auditor could not be confirmed from PureVPN's own pages.
- Trustpilot could not be fetched directly. Its figures come from a search-engine copy of the page.
