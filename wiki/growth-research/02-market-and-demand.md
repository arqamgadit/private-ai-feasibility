# 2. Market and demand structure

[Back to wiki home](../README.md) · [Back to the main report](00-main-report.md)

Research compiled **20 November 2026**. This page covers how big the consumer VPN market is, why people buy, what regulation is doing to demand, which platforms threaten the category, and how the review and affiliate economy shapes who wins.

## How to read the numbers

- **Sourced** figures carry an inline link to a page that was opened during this research.
- **Estimate** means our own calculation, with inputs and confidence stated.
- Several sources are review sites that earn affiliate commission or are owned by VPN companies. They are flagged where used.

## The short version

| Question | Answer | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| How big is the paid consumer VPN market? | About **$6B to $8B** a year in subscription revenue and roughly **60 to 120 million** paying users. Published "VPN market" figures of $60B to $90B mix in enterprise networking and are not this market. | Medium on revenue, low to medium on payers |
| How fast is it growing? | About **15% to 16%** a year. The leader grows faster: Nord Security's billings rose 29% in the year to June 2026. | Medium |
| Why do people buy? | Privacy and security first, then foreign streaming, then getting around blocks and age checks. | Medium |
| Is regulation helping? | Yes, in bursts. Age-verification laws and state blocks cause download spikes. Part of each spike fades. Free apps take a large share of it. | High on spikes, medium on what sticks |
| Who threatens the category? | Free VPNs inside browsers (Firefox, Opera), Apple's Private Relay, and security suites and telcos that bundle a VPN. | Medium |
| How does a challenger get seen? | Mostly by paying. Review sites earn commission, and some are owned by VPN groups. | High |

## 1. Market size: why published figures disagree

| Figure | Year | What it counts | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| $58.6B to $89.0B | 2025 | "VPN market" across several analyst houses. Includes enterprise VPN hardware, software and services | [Axis Intelligence roundup](https://axis-intelligence.com/vpn-statistics/) |
| $41.3B growing to $151.9B by 2030 | 2022 base | Same broad definition | [Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/virtual-private-network-market) |
| $28B, growing 16% a year to 2029 | 2025 | Consumer VPN "addressable market" as used by S&P in rating Nord Security. Addressable means what could be spent, not what is spent | [S&P Global Ratings, 8 Sep 2025](https://www.spglobal.com/ratings/en/regulatory/article/-/view/sourceId/101636953) |
| $5.9B, up 15.6% | 2024 | Revenue of consumer VPN apps | [Business of Apps](https://www.businessofapps.com/data/vpn-app-market/) |

The last line is the one to plan on. Growing $5.9B at 15% to 16% gives about $6.8B for 2025 and about $7.8B for 2026 (estimate).

### How many people pay

Nobody publishes a count of paying consumer VPN users. Two routes give a range.

| Route | Inputs | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Leader's share | Nord Security had more than 15 million paying subscribers and an estimated 15% to 25% share of its markets in September 2025 ([S&P](https://www.spglobal.com/ratings/en/regulatory/article/-/view/sourceId/101636953)) | 60M to 100M payers |
| Revenue divided by price | $5.9B of app revenue ([Business of Apps](https://www.businessofapps.com/data/vpn-app-market/)) at $50 to $60 a year per payer, which is what Nord earns ($878M of 2024 billings across about 15M subscribers, from the same S&P report) | 98M to 118M payers |

**Estimate: 60 to 120 million paying consumer VPN users worldwide. Confidence: low to medium.**

For context on total use, free and paid together:

- About 23% of internet users aged 16 and over said they used a VPN in the second quarter of 2025, in GWI's survey as reported in the Digital 2026 overview. Against 6.04 billion people online, that is about 1.4 billion people ([Browsec's review of the sources](https://browsec.com/en/blog/vpn-usage-statistics), a VPN vendor blog that traces each figure). The often-quoted "1.75 billion users" has no traceable method, according to the same review.
- About 150 million people used VPN apps in 2025 on Business of Apps' narrower count ([Business of Apps](https://www.businessofapps.com/data/vpn-app-market/)).
- S&P says free and freemium providers hold 60% to 75% of the market, and also that more than half of VPN users pay ([S&P](https://www.spglobal.com/ratings/en/regulatory/article/-/view/sourceId/101636953)). Those two statements sit awkwardly together. The first probably counts providers or installs and the second counts users in Nord's markets.

Source: [Browsec](https://browsec.com/en/blog/vpn-usage-statistics) and [VPNTex](https://vpntex.com/journal/vpn-adoption-asia-vs-europe-2026), both secondary summaries of GWI data; the country wave is several years old. In the United States, 42% of adults said they used a VPN in August 2026, up from 32% a year earlier ([Security.org survey of 1,008 adults](https://www.security.org/resources/vpn-consumer-report-annual/)).

The users are in Asia and the Middle East. The revenue is in North America and Europe. S&P notes that Nord Security earns "limited" revenue from emerging markets ([S&P](https://www.spglobal.com/ratings/en/regulatory/article/-/view/sourceId/101636953)). That gap is one of the few places a challenger is not fighting the leader head-on.

## 2. Why people buy

US adults who use a VPN, August 2026 ([Security.org](https://www.security.org/resources/vpn-consumer-report-annual/), review site's own survey):

| Reason | Share of VPN users |
|---|---|
| General privacy | 56% |
| General security | 51% |
| Access to international media | 27% |
| Reduce tracking | 27% |
| Hide activity from the internet provider | 25% |
| Access age-restricted or adult content | more than 10% |

Other findings from the same survey:

- 43% of VPN users use a free VPN.
- Among people who do not use a VPN, 39% say they do not need one, 24% say they do not know enough, and 18% say it is too expensive.
- NordVPN is used by 24% of VPN users and Proton VPN by 15%.

Streaming is larger in other surveys. A Tom's Guide survey of 1,400 people found 41% of subscribers used their VPN for streaming ([Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/features/more-than-40-of-vpn-subscribers-use-them-for-streaming-heres-why), a publication that earns affiliate commission).

**Reading.** The stated reason is privacy. The trigger to install is usually an event: a blocked site, an age check, a trip, a show that is not available locally. Product and marketing have to be ready for the trigger, and then give the buyer a reason to stay once the event has passed.

## 3. Regulatory demand shocks

### United Kingdom

| Event | What happened | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Online Safety Act age checks, 25 July 2025 | Daily users of VPN apps rose from about 650,000 to a peak above 1.4 million in mid-August 2025, then settled near 900,000 by November 2025 | [Ofcom Online Nation 2025](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/siteassets/resources/documents/research-and-data/online-research/online-nation/2025/online-nations-report-2025.pdf?v=409837), [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c39prelx2mxo) |
| Same event, app revenue | Combined UK downloads of the top five VPN apps rose from about 14,000 a day to nearly 70,000. Their combined net revenue rose from about $54,000 a day to a peak of $268,000 | [Appfigures, 8 Aug 2025](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20250808/amp?f=2) (Appfigures estimates) |
| Proposal to ban VPNs for under-18s | A House of Lords amendment to the Children's Wellbeing and Schools Bill. The Commons rejected it on 9 March 2026 by 321 votes to 106. The Act received Royal Assent on 29 April 2026 | [Amendment 92](https://bills.parliament.uk/bills/3909/stages/20215/amendments/10027478), [division record](https://ayesandnoes.co.uk/division/2270/children-s-wellbeing-and-schools-bill-motion-to-disagree-with-lords), [bill news](https://bills.parliament.uk/bills/3909/news) |
| Government position, July 2026 | "We will not ban or age-gate VPNs." Ofcom will research how platforms can detect circumvention, and the government says it is "prepared to take further action" | [UK government response](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/growing-up-in-the-online-world-a-national-consultation/outcome/growing-up-in-the-online-world-government-response-july-2026), [Biometric Update](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/uk-wont-ban-vpns-puts-onus-on-platforms-to-prevent-age-check-circumvention) |

The UK numbers are the clearest measure of a spike and its decay. Daily use more than doubled, and about a third of the uplift was still there three months later: 900,000 against a base of 650,000 and a peak of 1.4 million.

### Australia

- The under-16 social media ban took effect on 10 December 2025 and produced only a slight rise in VPN downloads ([Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/security/age-verification-laws-drive-surge-in-bypass-discussions-and-vpn-downloads/), a publication that earns affiliate commission).
- Age checks for adult content took effect on 9 March 2026. VPN downloads roughly tripled, with 871,000 in March 2026 ([Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/security/age-verification-laws-drive-surge-in-bypass-discussions-and-vpn-downloads/)). Proton VPN, NordVPN and a free app called VPN Super Unlimited Proxy all jumped into the top 20 apps ([The Guardian, 9 Mar 2026](https://www.theguardian.com/australia-news/2026/mar/09/vpn-downloads-australia-porn-sites-blocking-users)). Reuters noted that free VPNs ranked among the most popular apps ([Reuters, 9 Mar 2026](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/vpns-up-porn-websites-down-australia-brings-new-online-age-restrictions-2026-03-09/)).

### United States

- About half of US states now mandate age verification for some sites. Florida saw a 1,150% rise in VPN demand when its law took effect ([EFF, Dec 2025](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2025/12/year-states-chose-surveillance-over-safety-2025-review); the Florida figure originates with [vpnMentor](https://www.vpnmentor.com/news/vpn-and-adult-sites-report/), a review site owned by Kape, which also owns ExpressVPN).
- Bills that target VPNs directly have mostly failed so far. Wisconsin removed a VPN clause from its age-verification bill in February 2026 and the governor vetoed the bill on 3 April 2026 ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/vpn/vpn-privacy-security/wisconsin-scraps-vpn-ban-from-age-verification-bill-following-backlash), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/policy/907761/wisconsin-governor-porn-age-verification-vetoed)). Michigan's bill, which would make internet providers block VPNs, has not left committee ([isvpnlegal.com tracker](https://isvpnlegal.com/blog/us-vpn-laws/)). Utah passed a provision under which sites may not encourage VPN use; part of it has been blocked in court ([Biometric Update, Nov 2026](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202611/utah-separates-adult-site-age-checks-from-blocked-vpn-provision)).

### Countries that block

| Country | Situation | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Russia | The regulator restricted 469 VPN services by late February 2026. More than half of Russians aged 18 to 45 say they use a VPN (April 2026 survey). Russians downloaded VPN apps more than 9 million times in March 2026. The state target is to block 92% of VPN traffic by 2030 | [RIA Novosti, 26 Feb 2026](https://ria.ru/20260226/roskomnadzor-2076790707.html), [Carnegie, May 2026](https://carnegieendowment.org/russia-eurasia/politika/2026/05/russia-vpn-usage-political) |
| Iran | 74.4% of internet users use a circumvention tool and 66.7% use a free one, in a June 2026 survey commissioned by the Ministry of ICT (4,060 internet users, face to face) | [ISPA survey, archived English translation](https://sabt.it/archive/1784709827.529048/output.pdf) |
| Pakistan | The telecom regulator began licensing local VPN providers on 13 November 2025. Unlicensed services have been throttled or blocked in waves; Proton VPN reported restrictions from 22 December 2025. PureVPN is not among the named licensees | [PTA announcement](https://www.pta.gov.pk/category/pta-commences-licensing-of-vpn-service-providers-under-cvas-data-regime-1566349483-2025-11-13), [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/vpn/vpn-privacy-security/pakistan-begins-blocking-unregistered-vpn-apps-and-this-popular-service-is-among-the-casualties) |
| India | Rules issued in April 2022 require VPN providers with servers in India to keep customer records for five years. The large providers removed their Indian servers instead | [vpnandprivacy.com](https://vpnandprivacy.com/vpn/india) (review site) |
| Many others | Proton VPN recorded sign-up spikes of more than 100% in 62 countries in 2025, usually after a shutdown or a platform block. A Telegram block in Jordan on 16 November 2026 raised sign-ups 450% | [Proton VPN 2025 report](https://protonvpn.com/blog/eoy-report-2025), [Technobezz](https://www.technobezz.com/news/proton-vpn-signups-jordan-blocks-telegram) |

**Reading.** Blocks create the largest pools of VPN users in the world, and those users mostly choose free tools. A provider that wants them needs a free or very cheap tier, protocols that survive blocking, and distribution that survives app-store removal. It also takes on legal exposure and payment problems in sanctioned markets. Pakistan is a special case for PureVPN because most of its staff are there: a licence would bring obligations that may conflict with a no-logs promise, and going without one risks disruption at home.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Opera | Free browser VPN with three locations. Paid VPN Pro at $3.33 a month. A 30-day, no-sign-up trial of VPN Pro on Android in January 2026 | More than 400,000 activations in about a month | Free tier covers the browser only | [Opera feature page](https://www.opera.com/features/vpn), [Opera blog, 11 Mar 2026](https://blogs.opera.com/mobile/2026/03/vpn-pro-opera-for-android-pilot/) |

| Apple iCloud Private Relay | Included in iCloud+ from $0.99 a month. | Google One VPN | Bundled into Google One plans | Shut down in June 2024. Google said people were not using it | No location switching | [9to5Google, Jun 2024](https://9to5google.com/2024/06/21/vpn-by-google-one-shutdown/), [9to5Google, Apr 2024](https://9to5google.com/2024/04/11/google-one-vpn-discontinued/) |
| Microsoft Defender VPN | Bundled into Microsoft 365 | Ended 28 February 2025 | Low use | [Microsoft support](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/defender/end-of-support-privacy-protection-vpn-in-microsoft-defender-for-individuals) |
| Security suites | Norton, Avast and LifeLock owner Gen Digital has 79 million paid customers and $863M of partner revenue | Very large | VPN is one feature among many | [Gen Digital, 7 May 2026](https://newsroom.gendigital.com/2026-05-07-Gen-Crosses-5B-in-FY26-Revenue-with-Growth-Accelerating-to-Double-Digits) |
| Telco and bank bundles | Carriers and banks sell or include subscriptions. Bango's platform managed 24 million active subscriptions in 2025 and counts 7 of the top 8 US telcos as customers. Revolut includes NordVPN in its Ultra plan | Growing | The partner owns the customer | [Bango results, 27 Apr 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/27/3281314/0/en/Full-Year-Results-for-the-year-ended-31-December-2025.html), [Bango on Nord, 26 Jan 2026](https://bango.com/partner-spotlight-nord-security/) |

**Reading.**

1. A bundled VPN with no location choice failed twice at very large companies (Google and Microsoft). People install a VPN to appear somewhere else or to get past a block. Encryption alone does not hold them.
2. Free VPNs inside browsers are a live threat to the low end. Firefox and Opera both reached hundreds of thousands of activations within weeks by removing sign-up friction.
3. Bundles through telcos, banks and device makers are a threat if a rival wins the slot. They are an opportunity if PureVPN does. PureVPN already holds one such slot with Samsung.

## 5. Commoditisation

- Entry prices have converged at $2 to $4 a month on two-year plans: PureVPN $2.15 ([pricing](https://www.purevpn.com/pricing)), Surfshark $2.49 ([pricing](https://surfshark.com/pricing)), NordVPN $3.39 ([pricing](https://nordvpn.com/pricing/)), ExpressVPN $3.49 at its tier launch ([ExpressVPN, 2 Sep 2025](https://www.expressvpn.com/news-pr/expressvpn-launches-tiers)).
- Features that used to differentiate are now standard. Post-quantum encryption shipped across NordVPN's apps in May 2025 ([Nord Security](https://nordsecurity.com/press-area/nordvpn-launches-post-quantum-encryption-across-all-its-applications/)), is on by default at Mullvad ([Mullvad](https://mullvad.net/en/blog/mullvad-review-of-2025)) and at ExpressVPN ([Lightway](https://www.expressvpn.com/lightway)).
- S&P describes the industry as having low barriers to entry and says growth relies on expensive channels: affiliates, influencers and pay-per-click ([S&P](https://www.spglobal.com/ratings/en/regulatory/article/-/view/sourceId/101636953)). Fitch expects higher customer acquisition costs to hold back Nord's margins ([Fitch, 20 Oct 2026](https://www.fitchratings.com/research/corporate-finance/fitch-affirms-cyberspace-bv-nord-security-idr-at-bb-outlook-stable-20-10-2026); the page returned only navigation when fetched directly, so its content was read from the search-engine copy).
- The response of every large provider has been to bundle. About half of Nord's new web subscribers take two or more products ([S&P](https://www.spglobal.com/ratings/en/regulatory/article/-/view/sourceId/101636953)), and non-VPN products passed 20% of its billings by June 2026 ([Fitch](https://www.fitchratings.com/research/corporate-finance/fitch-affirms-cyberspace-bv-nord-security-idr-at-bb-outlook-stable-20-10-2026)).

## 6. Review sites and affiliate economics

| Review property | Owner | VPNs the owner also sells | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| vpnMentor, WizCase | Kape Technologies, through Webselenese | ExpressVPN, CyberGhost, Private Internet Access | [vpnMentor's own disclosure](https://www.vpnmentor.com/about-us/) |
| PCMag, Mashable, CNET | Ziff Davis. CNET was bought in 2024 for more than $100M | IPVanish | [New York Times, 6 Aug 2024](https://www.nytimes.com/2024/08/06/business/media/cnet-ziff-davis.html), [CleanVPNs ownership guide](https://cleanvpns.com/guides/who-owns-your-vpn/) |
| Comparitech | Point Wild | Hotspot Shield | [Boycat ownership guide](https://www.boycat.io/vpn/guides/vpn-review-sites-ownership) |
| TechRadar, Tom's Guide | Future plc | None, but both earn affiliate commission | [SafePaper](https://safepaper.io/vpn/vpn-relationship/) |

How the money works:

- Most review sites are paid when a reader buys through their link. Providers with no affiliate programme, such as Mullvad, rarely appear on monetised lists ([Boycat](https://www.boycat.io/vpn/guides/vpn-review-sites-ownership), [Mullvad's policy](https://mullvad.net/en/blog/advertising-that-targets-everyone)).
- PureVPN pays 100% of the first month on monthly plans and 40% on longer plans, plus 35% of renewals ([PureVPN affiliates](https://www.purevpn.com/affiliates)). Proton pays on similar headline terms ([ServiceAnatomy](https://serviceanatomy.com/en/articles/proton), a secondary source). So the commission rate is not where PureVPN loses.
- A site ranks by expected earnings per click: commission multiplied by how often readers buy. A brand that readers already know converts better, so it earns the site more at the same rate. PureVPN's US brand search volume is about one fiftieth of NordVPN's ([baseline page](01-purevpn-baseline.md#7-traffic-and-acquisition-mix)).

**Estimate of what acquisition costs at scale.** Kape, the last large VPN group to publish accounts, paid $233.4M in cash for marketing costs to obtain customer contracts in 2022 and ended the year with 7.36 million subscribers, up 791,000 ([Kape full-year results 2022](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H22_AN202303211584441241_1.pdf)). If 25% to 30% of its opening base churned in the year, gross additions were about 2.4 to 2.8 million and the cost per new subscriber was about **$85 to $95**. Confidence is low, because the churn rate is our assumption. Kape reports retention only on a six-month basis (83%). For a different category, the identity-protection company Aura reports a cost of $171 to $192 per new direct subscriber ([Aura business overview, 26 Mar 2026](https://qoria.com/hubfs/Qoria/Corporate/ASX-Announcements%20(KEEP)/2026/2026-03-26_Merger%20Update%20and%20Aura%20Business%20Overview.pdf)).

## Open gaps

- No source gives paying consumer VPN users directly. The 60 to 120 million range rests on Nord's share and price.
- Country-level adoption figures come from an old GWI wave relayed by secondary sites.
- Retention after regulatory spikes is measured only for the UK, and only as app usage, not paid subscriptions.
- The Fitch note was read from a search-engine copy because the page returned only navigation when fetched. The S&P report was read directly.
