# 3. Competitor product strategy teardown

[Back to wiki home](../README.md) · [Back to the main report](00-main-report.md)

Research compiled **20 November 2026**. This page asks one question of each competitor: which product moves added customers, which did not, and what that means for PureVPN. Private-AI competitors are covered in depth in the [Venice note](../competitor-research/venice-ai.md) and [page 7](../07-market-research.md); this page only links that work to VPN growth.

## How to read the numbers

- **Sourced** figures carry an inline link to a page opened during this research.
- **Claim** means a company's statement about itself.
- **Estimate** means our calculation, with inputs.
- Tool data comes from free public Semrush pages (October 2026 data, pulled 20 November 2026) and from store listings pulled the same day.

### What worked

- **The core engine is marketing, not features.** S&P says Nord grows through affiliates, influencers and pay-per-click, and recovers acquisition cost within "several months" because customers prepay one or two years ([S&P](https://www.spglobal.com/ratings/en/regulatory/article/-/view/sourceId/101636953)). Its site drew 1.13M paid-search visits in October 2026 against PureVPN's 362 ([Semrush, nordvpn.com](https://www.semrush.com/website/nordvpn.com/overview/), [Semrush, purevpn.com](https://www.semrush.com/website/purevpn.com/overview/)).
- **Bundles raise value per customer.** About half of new web subscribers take two or more products. Non-VPN products passed 20% of billings by June 2026 (S&P and Fitch, linked above).
- **Saily is the clearest case of a new product adding new customers.** A team of seven or eight built it in 19 weeks. It reached 1 million users by the end of 2024 and about 4 million by February 2026 with a team of about 100 ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/communications-tech/founding-startup-inside-scale-up-maknickas-saily/), [PEGN, Feb 2026](https://revistapegn.globo.com/startups/noticia/2026/02/como-este-app-nascido-dentro-de-um-unicornio-cresceu-4x-em-um-ano.ghtml)). Its website drew 2.5M visits in October 2026 and ranks first in the US for the search "esim" ([Semrush, saily.com](https://www.semrush.com/website/saily.com/overview/)). A traveller who has never wanted a VPN will buy an eSIM. Nord's paying-user count rose from more than 15 million to more than 22 million in under a year, and Saily's users are probably part of that.
- **Incogni built a brand of its own.** "incogni" gets 90,500 US searches a month, about 17 times "purevpn" ([Semrush, incogni.com](https://www.semrush.com/website/incogni.com/overview/)).
- **Retention is high for the category.** 79% of users were retained at June 2026 (Fitch).

### What did not work

- **Meshnet.** Only about 1% of subscribers used it. Nord announced its shutdown in August 2025 because it slowed work on the core app, then reversed after a backlash and promised to open-source it ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/vpn/vpn-services/its-official-nordvpns-meshnet-is-not-going-anywhere), [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/computing/vpns/nordvpn-u-turns-on-axing-meshnet-due-to-public-backlash)).
- **Emerging markets.** Revenue there is "limited" (S&P), even though Nord prices at 35% of the US level in India ([EasyPPP price tracker, 31 Oct 2026](https://www.easyppp.com/ppp-tracker/nordvpn)).

### Lessons for PureVPN

1. Nord's method needs Nord's budget. Copying the feature list without the marketing engine has already been tried at PureVPN and has not moved the count.
2. A product with its own front door and its own search demand (Saily, Incogni) adds customers. The same product as a checkbox inside a VPN plan adds revenue per customer.
3. Features for enthusiasts cost more to maintain than they return.

## Surfshark

- **What it launched.** Unlimited devices on every plan. The lowest headline price of the large brands, $2.49 a month on a 27-month term. Alternative ID (a generated persona and email) in every plan. An Alternative Number add-on. Antivirus in the One tier. Incogni in One+. Scam-call protection on Android in October 2026 and iOS on 18 November 2026 ([Surfshark pricing](https://surfshark.com/pricing), [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/vpn/vpn-services/surfshark-updates-its-iphone-vpn-app-to-filter-out-scam-callers-but-you-need-a-premium-plan)).
- **Evidence it worked.** 4 million paying users and €149M of 2024 revenue, about €37 per user (estimate), with more than 500 employees ([LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/regimantasurbanas_if-you-invest-in-stocks-would-you-consider-activity-7434851400632868864-xOYF), [Surfshark about page](https://surfshark.com/about-us)). Surfshark buys traffic heavily: 296K paid-search visits in October 2026 ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/surfshark.com/overview/)).
- **Lesson.** Surfshark shows that a low-price challenger can reach millions. It did so with a simple, memorable hook (unlimited devices), consistent app quality (4.6 on Google Play), and paid acquisition. PureVPN has the low price. It lacks the hook, the app quality and the spend.

## Proton

- **What it launched.** A free VPN with unlimited data, no sign-up needed on Android, and servers in 10 countries ([Proton VPN Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ch.protonvpn.android&hl=en&gl=US), [Proton VPN pricing](https://protonvpn.com/pricing)). An ecosystem of mail, drive, password manager and calendar on one account. Control by a nonprofit foundation since 2024 ([Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/news/note/proton-100m-revenues-still-no-valuation-on-file)). Lumo, a private AI assistant, with version 2.0 on 30 June 2026 ([IntelligentHQ](https://www.intelligenthq.com/proton-launches-lumo-2-0-can-privacy-first-ai-finally-compete-with-chatgpt-and-claude/)). Anti-censorship tools: a Stealth protocol, alternative routing, and distribution outside app stores ([Proton VPN 2025 report](https://protonvpn.com/blog/eoy-report-2025)).
- **Evidence it worked.** More than 100 million accounts and more than $100M of revenue with 650 employees ([fDi Intelligence](https://www.fdiintelligence.com/content/f3d9fb74-b947-41df-ac19-26c2a4716151)). Proton VPN's Android app has 140 million downloads ([Chrome-Stats publisher page, 16 Nov 2026](https://chrome-stats.com/a/NzY3MjQ3OTcwNjU1ODUyNjY0Nw)). In a US survey, 15% of VPN users use Proton VPN, second only to NordVPN ([Security.org](https://www.security.org/resources/vpn-consumer-report-annual/)). Sign-ups spike whenever a government blocks something: 62 countries in 2025 ([Proton VPN 2025 report](https://protonvpn.com/blog/eoy-report-2025)).
- **Evidence on Lumo.** Proton says more than 10 million people have used it ([Proton Lumo page](https://proton.me/lumo), [IntelligentHQ](https://www.intelligenthq.com/proton-launches-lumo-2-0-can-privacy-first-ai-finally-compete-with-chatgpt-and-claude/)). Its Android app has about 414,000 downloads and a 3.8 rating ([Chrome-Stats](https://chrome-stats.com/a/NzY3MjQ3OTcwNjU1ODUyNjY0Nw)). Most use is therefore on the web, and much of it is likely existing Proton account holders trying it.
- **What it costs.** Proton does not publish how many free users convert or what they cost to serve. Revenue of a little over $100M on more than 100 million accounts is about $1 per account per year (estimate). The free tier pays because it feeds a whole ecosystem and because Proton's trust standing makes the upgrade credible.
- **Lesson.** A free tier is the only proven way to reach tens of millions of users without Nord-scale marketing. It works when the brand is trusted, the apps are good, and the service survives blocking. PureVPN does not yet meet those three conditions.

## ExpressVPN and Kape

- **What it launched.** Three tiers on 2 September 2025: Basic $3.49, Advanced $4.49, Pro $7.49 a month on two-year terms ([ExpressVPN](https://www.expressvpn.com/news-pr/expressvpn-launches-tiers)). The bundle now includes a password manager, email aliases (ExpressMailGuard), a private AI assistant (ExpressAI), Identity Defender for US customers with up to $5M of identity-theft insurance, and travel eSIM data from holiday.com ([ExpressVPN, five protections](https://www.expressvpn.com/news-pr/one-subscription-five-protections), [plan list](https://www.expressvpn.com/support/manage-account/available-subscriptions/)). ExpressAI launched on 31 March 2026 ([Venice note](../competitor-research/venice-ai.md)) and the plan list shows it in every tier.
- **Hardware.** The Aircove router was unveiled in 2022 ([Kape 2022 results](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H22_AN202303211584441241_1.pdf)). ExpressVPN retired Aircove and Aircove Go on 16 September 2026 and now sells a router built by GL.iNet instead ([ExpressVPN](https://www.expressvpn.com/news-pr/expressvpn-fortify-glinet-router)).
- **Ownership.** Kape bought ExpressVPN in 2021 for about $936M. Kape was taken private in 2023 by its majority owner and cut about 12% of staff soon after ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ExpressVPN), [Privacy Journal](https://www.privacyjournal.net/kape-technologies/)). This research found no ownership change in 2026.
- **Marketing.** ExpressVPN is moving sponsorship money out of live sport and into esports ([City AM](https://www.cityam.com/spurs-face-sponsor-uncertainty-as-expressvpn-push-esports-pivot/)).
- **Evidence.** Kape has published no subscriber figure since 7.4 million at the end of 2022 ([Kape 2022 results](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H22_AN202303211584441241_1.pdf)). There is therefore no public evidence that the 2025–26 bundle added customers. Brand demand is still strong: "expressvpn" gets 135,000 US searches a month ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/expressvpn.com/overview/)).
- **Lesson.** The premium brand has added tiers, an AI assistant, identity cover and an eSIM in 14 months. Each PureVPN bundle feature now has a direct counterpart at a better-known brand. Owning hardware did not last even for a company of Kape's size.

## Mullvad

- **What it does.** One product, one price (€5 a month), no affiliates, no paid reviews, no influencers, anonymous numbered accounts, and outdoor advertising about surveillance ([Mullvad advertising policy](https://mullvad.net/en/blog/advertising-that-targets-everyone), [Why Mullvad](https://mullvad.net/en/why-mullvad-vpn)). Reproducible Android builds and post-quantum tunnels by default ([Mullvad review of 2025](https://mullvad.net/en/blog/mullvad-review-of-2025)).
- **Evidence it worked.** Revenue grew from SEK 97.0M in 2021 to SEK 380.9M in 2025, up 21% in the last year, with 53 employees and an operating profit of SEK 62.3M ([Ratsit](https://www.ratsit.se/5592384001-Mullvad_VPN_AB), [rating.se](https://www.rating.se/info1/detail/numbers/5592384001)).
- **Lesson.** Trust alone can build a profitable business of roughly PureVPN's size with a fraction of the staff. It does not produce ten-fold growth: Mullvad roughly quadrupled revenue in four years from a small base and is still under 0.6 million accounts.

## Windscribe

- **What it launched.** A free plan with 10 GB a month for confirmed email addresses, extra data for posting about the service, a referral programme, and "build a plan" pricing at $1 per location per month with a $3 minimum ([Windscribe pricing article](https://windscribe.com/knowledge-base/articles/how-much-does-it-cost-to-use-windscribe), [free plan article](https://windscribe.com/knowledge-base/articles/can-i-use-windscribe-for-free)).
- **Evidence.** More than 100 million registered users ([The Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/politics/article-online-privacy-windscribe-lawful-access-bill/)). In 2022 the founder described 43 million users, about $3M of annual revenue and a profitable company of 30 to 40 people ([founder interview](https://blog.jasonsherman.org/2022/07/20/zero-to-ceo-how-to-crush-your-competition-in-tech-with-a-small-team-with-yegor-sak/)). That is about seven cents of revenue per registered user per year (estimate).
- **Lesson.** A free tier can produce a very large user count and very little money. Windscribe has twenty times PureVPN's claimed users and, on the 2022 figures, a fraction of its revenue. If "customers" means paying customers, free users only count once they convert.

## Private Internet Access and CyberGhost

Both belong to Kape. Their websites drew 2.15M and 2.03M visits in October 2026 ([Semrush, PIA](https://www.semrush.com/website/privateinternetaccess.com/overview/), [Semrush, CyberGhost](https://www.semrush.com/website/cyberghostvpn.com/overview/)). This research found no recent product launch at either brand with evidence of customer growth, and Kape no longer publishes brand-level numbers. We draw no lesson from them beyond this: both are larger than PureVPN on web traffic while doing little that is new, which says brand age and owned review sites still carry weight.

## What drove customer growth, in order of evidence

1. **Paid acquisition at scale, financed by prepaid multi-year plans.** Nord, Surfshark, Kape. Strong evidence. Not open to PureVPN at its current brand strength without large capital.
2. **A free tier backed by trust.** Proton. Strong evidence on users, weak public evidence on paying conversion.
3. **Partner distribution.** Gen and F-Secure in security; Samsung, Revolut and telco bundles in VPN. Strong evidence on customer count, lower revenue per customer.
4. **A new product with its own front door.** Saily, Incogni. Good evidence, but both rode Nord's marketing engine.
5. **Trust-led word of mouth.** Mullvad. Good evidence of profitable growth, no evidence of ten-fold scale.
6. **Bundle features inside the VPN plan.** Everyone. Good evidence for revenue per customer and retention. No evidence they add customers.

## Open gaps

- No public data on how many free Proton users convert to paid, or what a free user costs to serve.
- No subscriber data for Kape brands after 2022, or for Incogni, DeleteMe and DuckDuckGo's subscription.
- Saily "users" are people who bought at least once. Repeat rates are not public.
- Paid Semrush and Ahrefs data (keyword-level paid spend, historical trends) was not available. Only free overview pages were used.
