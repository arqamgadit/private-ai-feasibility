# Cloaked vs competitors (incl. Nord & Express)

[Back to wiki home](../README.md)

Detailed feature comparison of **Cloaked** against identity-masking, data-removal, and ID-theft suites, with explicit mapping of **Nord Security** and **ExpressVPN** offerings. Compiled **24 November 2026**.

Interactive version: [Cloaked vs competitors feature comparison](https://cursor.com/canvas/store-6d9201ae-7bc3-4f73-868f-a4721e776fc1/4e85656c-5320-4007-a49c-341217fcbfb8).

## What Cloaked is

Cloaked sells a **prevention-first identity privacy suite** (US/Canada-oriented):

| Layer | Features |
|---|---|
| Prevention | Unlimited email aliases; unlimited phone aliases (VoIP); optional **Cloaked eSIM** (US carrier-backed number); **Cloaked Pay** virtual cards (US, SSN KYC); password manager + TOTP per identity |
| Interception | **Call Guard** (US): carrier call-forwarding + AI screens unknowns; Text/Email Guard |
| Cleanup | Automated data-broker / people-search removal (marketing often says “1000+”; Cloaked’s own comparison page cites ~140+ brokers — treat counts carefully) |
| Monitor | Dark web & SSN alerts; Cloaked’s comparison page also claims credit / bank alerts (verify on live plan card) |
| Recover | Up to **$1M** identity-theft insurance (AIG), with paid subscription |
| Network | Included encrypted VPN |

Pricing (vendor plans page / third-party mirrors, Nov 2026): Individual **$14.99/mo** or **$119.99/yr** (~$9.99/mo); Couple **$19.99 / $174.99**; Family **$29.99 / $299.99**. Free risk scan; no full free trial (limited unpaid password/email window only).

## Nord vs Express vs Cloaked

Neither Nord nor Express ships Cloaked’s full “new phone + email + card per signup” stack as one first-party product. Express is the closer US suite rival; Nord is stronger on email/synthetic identity masking + partnered removal.

| Capability | Cloaked | ExpressVPN suite | Nord Security suite |
|---|---|---|---|
| How you buy it | Standalone Individual / Couple / Family | Bundled: MailGuard on all tiers; Keys + Identity Defender on Advanced/Pro (ID Defender **US only**) | NordPass Premium for masking; Incogni via **Ultra** (select markets) or add-on; **Prime / NordProtect** in US |
| Email aliases | Unlimited + dedicated inboxes | ExpressMailGuard: unlimited aliases; shared aliases / custom domains scale by tier | NordPass Email Masking (Premium+) |
| Phone aliases | Unlimited VoIP + optional US eSIM | **No.** Pro US: Caller Identification on the **real** line | **No** first-party phone aliases. Scam Call Protection on higher VPN plans. Incogni Concierge (separate) can include secure phone |
| Synthetic name/address | Per-identity Cloaked profiles | Not a first-party generator | **NordPass Identity Masking** (Oct 2026): synthetic name + address + masked email |
| Virtual cards | Cloaked Pay (US) | No (Keys stores real cards) | No (NordPass stores real cards) |
| Spam/scam call AI | Call Guard (US primary line) | Caller ID on Express Pro US | Scam Call Protection (market / plan dependent) |
| Data broker removal | Automated (count claims inconsistent) | Identity Defender Data Removal (US) | Incogni Standard (**420+**) on Ultra / add-on |
| Dark web / SSN | Yes | Advanced: SSN, dark web, change-of-address. Pro adds title, court, 401(k), high-risk txns | Via NordPass monitors + NordProtect/Prime; Incogni Protect (US) |
| Credit monitoring | Claimed on Cloaked comparison page — verify | Credit Scanner (Experian) | Via Protect/Prime, not basic NordPass |
| ID theft insurance | Up to $1M (AIG) | Advanced up to **$3M** · Pro up to **$5M** (Assurant) | ~$1M via NordProtect / Incogni Protect (Coveron) |
| Password manager | Included | ExpressKeys (Advanced/Pro or standalone) | NordPass (Plus+ VPN or standalone) |
| VPN | Included | Core product | Core product |
| Approx. paid signal | $14.99/mo or $119.99/yr | Advanced ~$4.49/mo · Pro ~$7.49/mo on 2-yr promo (whole suite) | NordPass street price; Ultra intro often ~€6–9/mo regional; Incogni Standard alone ~$7.99/mo |

### Express pieces that map to Cloaked

- **ExpressMailGuard** ↔ Cloaked email aliases (Express is strong on custom domains at Pro).
- **ExpressKeys** ↔ Cloaked password manager.
- **Identity Defender** ↔ Cloaked removal + monitoring + insurance (Express often **deeper** on credit/title/court and **higher** insurance caps).
- **Caller Identification (Pro US)** ↔ Cloaked Call Guard only partially (screens/blocks spam on your real number; does **not** issue disposable numbers).
- **Gaps:** no phone aliases, no virtual cards, no Cloaked-style identity-per-merchant profiles.

### Nord pieces that map to Cloaked

- **NordPass Email Masking + Identity Masking** ↔ Cloaked email aliases + synthetic identity fill (Nord’s Oct 2026 Identity Masking is the closest Nord feature to Cloaked’s “fake persona for forms”).
- **Incogni (Ultra / add-on)** ↔ Cloaked data removal (Incogni is the cleanup specialist Nord white-labels).
- **NordProtect / Prime (US)** ↔ Cloaked dark-web/SSN + ~$1M recovery layer.
- **Scam Call Protection** ↔ partial Call Guard analogue on the real line.
- **Gaps:** no first-party phone aliases, no virtual cards; features fragmented across apps and **country-specific plan names** (Ultra vs Prime vs Ultimate).

## Where each wins

| Job | Winner |
|---|---|
| Phone + email prevention in one app | **Cloaked** (IronVest/Ivy runner-up) |
| Closest VPN-suite Cloaked substitute (US) | **Express Advanced/Pro** |
| Email + synthetic form identity via password manager | **NordPass Identity Masking** |
| Broker scrub on a budget | **Incogni** |
| Thorough / transparent removal | **Optery** or **DeleteMe** (depending on human vs automated preference) |
| Credit + family + device security | **Aura** |
| Highest ID insurance caps in a VPN suite | **Express Pro** ($5M marketed) |
| Virtual cards only | **Privacy.com** or **Cloaked Pay** / IronVest |

## Bottom line

Cloaked’s moat is **prevention with real contact aliases** — especially **phone numbers**, **Call Guard**, and **virtual cards** — not insurance caps or “1000+ sites” marketing. **Express** is the closest VPN-brand package for US users (aliases + removal + deep monitoring + high insurance) but still protects your **real** phone rather than replacing it. **Nord** covers email/identity masking and Incogni-based cleanup well, and is farthest from Cloaked on **phone aliases** and **masked payments**.
