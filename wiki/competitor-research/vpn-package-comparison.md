# PureVPN vs ExpressVPN vs Proton: product and pricing comparison

[Back to wiki home](../README.md)

Checked **15 November 2026** from each vendor's pricing and plan pages. Introductory VPN prices move with seasonal sales and A/B tests; treat first-term figures as a snapshot and re-check before any packaging decision. All amounts are **USD** unless noted. Proton also publishes euro list prices; US checkout often shows the dollar figures below.

## Why this matters here

This wiki is for a Venice-style private AI product that PureVPN could attach. Two of the three peers already sell **private AI inside the VPN / privacy bill**:

| Vendor | Private AI in the VPN bill today |
|---|---|
| **PureVPN** | No — Standard / Plus / Max are VPN + optional privacy tools |
| **ExpressVPN** | Yes — **ExpressAI** on every tier (credits and models scale with plan) |
| **Proton** | Partially — **Lumo Free** on paid plans; **Lumo Plus** is a paid add-on ($12.99/mo or $9.99/mo yearly), included only on **Visionary** |

ExpressVPN is the closest packaging precedent for "VPN + private AI as one SKU." Proton is the closest precedent for a full privacy suite with AI as a separate upsell.

Best published first-term monthly rate for a full-featured personal VPN (not free tiers). PureVPN's Black Friday 5-year SKU is included because it is live on the order page today.

## 1. PureVPN packages

### Personal tiers

All three share the same VPN network and **10 simultaneous devices** (Multi-Login add-on can raise that toward 50). Differentiator is the surrounding privacy toolkit, not VPN speed or server access.

| Tracker / ad blocker | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Password manager (PureKeep) | — | Yes | Yes |

| Dark web monitoring | — | — | Yes (full; lower tiers may show limited breach counts) |
| Travel eSIM | — | — | Yes (order page: ~3 GB / 30 days) |

### Network and product notes

- About **6,000+ servers** in **65+ countries** (marketing pages also say 80+ locations).
- WireGuard, OpenVPN, IKEv2; kill switch; split tunneling (platform-dependent); obfuscated servers.
- Paid add-ons (not in base tiers): dedicated IP, port forwarding, dedicated server, residential network (US/UK, bandwidth-capped from ~12 GB), Multi-Login, DDoS-protected VPN.
- **Teams** plan exists for business (third-party reviews ~$7/user/mo yearly) — separate from consumer Standard/Plus/Max.

### Packaging posture

Cheap, long-commitment VPN with optional identity/password extras. **No private AI SKU.** Room to attach AI without colliding with an existing ExpressAI-style product, but PureVPN must still compete with ExpressVPN's bundled AI and Proton's Lumo.

## 2. ExpressVPN packages

### Personal tiers

Three consumer tiers. Every tier includes the VPN, **Advanced Protection** (ads / trackers / malicious / adult-site blocking), **ExpressMailGuard** (email relay), and **ExpressAI**. Higher tiers add password manager, identity defense (US), eSIM, more devices, and a dedicated IP on Pro.

| | **Basic** | **Advanced** | **Express Pro** |
|---|---|---|---|
| VPN (Lightway, up to 40 Gbps claimed) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Countries | 113 | 113 | 113 |
| Simultaneous devices | 10 | 12 | 14 |
| Dedicated IP | Add-on | Add-on | **Included** |
| ExpressAI | 50 credits/day, 1 model, 100 MB storage | 100 credits/day, 1 model, 500 MB | 500 credits/day, **6 models**, 2 GB |
| ExpressMailGuard | 10 MB/mo relay, 10 shared aliases | 100 MB/mo, 50 aliases, 1 custom domain | Unlimited relay/aliases, 20 custom domains |
| ExpressKeys (password manager) | — | Yes (5 GB attachments) | Yes (10 GB) |
| Identity Defender | — | Limited (e.g. 5 alerts, data removal, up to $3M ID theft insurance) | Full suite (up to $5M insurance) |
| holiday.com eSIM | — | 3 GB / 3 days (long plans) | 3 GB / 5 days (long plans) |
| Money-back | 30 days (new users) | 30 days | 30 days |

Identity Defender is **US-only** per ExpressVPN's subscription docs. eSIM appears on 12-month and 2-year Advanced/Pro offers, not on monthly Advanced.

### Pricing (pricing page + support docs, Nov 2026)

Introductory 2-year Advanced/Pro prices A/B-test between roughly **$4.19–4.49** and **$7.19–7.49**. Figures below use the range where needed.

| Term | Basic | Advanced | Express Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monthly** | $14.99 | $16.99 | $22.99 |
| **1 year** | ~$4.99/mo | ~$5.99/mo | ~$8.99/mo |
| **2 years + 4 months (28 mo)** | **$2.99/mo** ($83.72 billed) | **$4.19–4.49/mo** ($117–126 billed) | **$7.19–7.49/mo** ($201–210 billed) |
| **Renewal (after intro)** | $99.95 / year | $119.95 / year | $199.95 / year |

### Packaging posture

Premium VPN brand that has already moved to a **cybersecurity suite + private AI**. Basic alone undercuts buying VPN + a separate AI sub for light use. Pro is the "everything" SKU (dedicated IP + heavy ExpressAI). This is the packaging PureVPN would be answering if it ships a private AI attach on Max or a new AI tier.

## 3. Proton packages

Proton sells **standalone product Pluses**, **VPN Plus**, **suite bundles**, **multi-user plans**, and **Lumo** separately. You cannot stack multiple Plus products on one account (e.g. VPN Plus + Mail Plus); Unlimited / Duo / Family / Visionary unlock the suite.

### VPN-focused

| Plan | What you get | Monthly | Yearly (intro / typical) | 2-year | Renewal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **VPN Free** | 1 device, medium speed, ~10 countries auto-chosen, unlimited data | $0 | — | — | — |
| **VPN Plus** | 10 devices, highest speed, 20,000+ servers / 140+ countries, NetShield, streaming, P2P, Secure Core, Tor-over-VPN | $9.99 | ~$3.99/mo ($47.88) or promo ~$2.99 ($35.88) | **$2.99/mo** (~$71.76) | $83.88 / year |

VPN Plus unlocks free-tier Mail / Drive / Pass / Calendar only — not the paid Plus features of those apps.

### Standalone product Pluses (often discounted yearly)

| Plan | List monthly | Current yearly intro (approx.) | Renews (yearly) | Core unlock |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mail Plus** | $4.99 | $2.49/mo ($29.88 first year) | $47.88/yr | 15 GB, 10 addresses, 1 custom domain, Bridge, dark-web monitoring |
| **Pass Plus** | $4.99 | $1.99/mo ($23.88 first year) | $35.88/yr | Unlimited aliases, 2FA, vaults, dark-web monitoring |
| **Pass Family** | $6.99 | $2.79/mo ($33.48 first year) | $59.88/yr | Pass Plus × up to 6 |
| **Drive Plus** | $4.99 | $2.49/mo ($29.88 first year) | $47.88/yr | 200 GB encrypted storage |
| **Lumo Plus** | $12.99 | $9.99/mo ($119.88/yr) | $119.88/yr | Higher Max-model usage, history, images; unlimited Projects / Custom Lumos |

Buying Mail + Pass + Drive + VPN + Lumo as separate Pluses is far more expensive than Unlimited; Proton's design pushes the suite.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Unlimited** | 1 | 500 GB | Yes | Mail/Pass/Drive/Calendar/Wallet Plus; **Lumo Free** | $12.99 | $6.49/mo ($77.88) | $119.88/yr (~$9.99/mo) |

Support docs still cite older server counts (15,000+ / 120+ countries) in places; the VPN pricing page currently markets **20,000+ / 140+**.

### Packaging posture

Swiss open-source privacy ecosystem. VPN can be free, VPN-only, or free inside a much larger encrypted productivity suite. **Private AI (Lumo) is not the hero of Unlimited** — it is free-capped unless the user pays Lumo Plus or holds Visionary. That leaves a wedge for a PureVPN AI attach that is more generous than Lumo Free and cheaper than Lumo Plus + a separate VPN.

## 5. Private AI packaging comparison (relevant to this project)

| | PureVPN | ExpressVPN ExpressAI | Proton Lumo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Included with base VPN? | No | Yes (all tiers) | Free capped on paid Proton; guest free without account |
| Paid AI SKU | None yet | Upsell via higher VPN tier (more credits/models) | Lumo Plus $12.99/mo or $9.99/mo yearly |
| Privacy claim | — | Confidential computing / "truly private AI" | Zero-access encryption; chats not used for training |
| Usage meter | — | Daily credits (50 / 100 / 500) | Max-model / message / image caps; Plus raises them |
| Models | — | 1 model on Basic/Advanced; 6 on Pro | Lite / Max / Apertus (routing not fully open) |
| Bundle economics | Opportunity | AI is a reason to buy Advanced/Pro | Lumo Plus often bought *on top of* Unlimited (~$6.49–9.99 + $9.99) |

**Implication for PureVPN attach pricing (planning judgment, not a decision):**

1. A PureVPN **AI add-on near $5–10/mo** or a **Max+AI tier near Express Advanced ($4–5/mo long-term)** sits in a crowded but understandable band.
2. Beating ExpressVPN requires either **cheaper AI on the entry VPN** or **clearer privacy / model / agent capabilities** than ExpressAI's credit tiers.
3. Beating Proton means being better than **Lumo Free** without forcing users into **Lumo Plus + VPN**. Unlimited customers already pay for privacy apps; they may still pay for AI if limits bite.
4. PureVPN's structural advantage remains **distribution and low VPN ARPU**; AI should raise attach ARPU without wrecking the $2-ish VPN price perception.

## 6. What each vendor is optimizing for

| Vendor | Pricing strategy | Product strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **PureVPN** | Deep multi-year discounts; low renewal vs peers; trial that converts to annual | VPN-first; sell Plus/Max and add-ons; expand device count via Multi-Login |
| **ExpressVPN** | High monthly list; aggressive 28-month intro; steep renewal jump | Premium VPN → full cyber suite with AI as a first-class included feature |
| **Proton** | Free funnel; many SKUs; Unlimited as the value hero; household Duo/Family | Encrypted productivity suite; VPN is one pillar; AI is adjacent (Lumo), not the VPN SKU itself |
