# 7. Venice.ai competitor analysis

[Back to wiki home](../README.md)

Research compiled **28 October 2026**. Traffic, revenue and token figures move fast, so re-check before using them externally.

## Framing

Venice.ai is treated here as a **competitor to PureVPN's private-AI plans**, not as an acquisition target. It is a privacy-first, "unrestricted" AI platform founded in 2024 by Erik Voorhees (ShapeShift), with Jesse Proudman as co-founder and CTO. It raised a $65M Series A at a $1B valuation in July 2026 (Dragonfly lead) and is profitable.

How to read the numbers:

- **Sourced** figures have a link in the Sources section.
- **Estimate** means our own calculation or judgment. Each one states its assumptions and confidence.

### Channels ranked by importance (our judgment)

1. Founder brand (Voorhees, about 1M X followers) plus funding press.
2. SEO aimed at "uncensored", "private" and "alternative to" searches.

4. Crypto community: VVV airdrop (50% of the original supply), staking, DIEM. Discord has about 17K members.
5. Developer and agent distribution: OpenAI-compatible API, OpenRouter, Fleek ($10 free credits), OpenClaw skills, Circle Agent Marketplace, a $27M incentive fund.
6. App stores: iOS 4.3★ (1.6K ratings), Google Play 4.1★ (about 4.8K reviews), about 1M+ lifetime installs (AppInsider estimate).
7. Referral programme: "give $10, get $10" in credits.
8. PR and events: the Lumara AI film festival with MoonPay, with prizes of $8–12K plus 0.8–1.2M credits.

### The pitch

- **"Like a VPN, but for AI"** on [venice.ai/lp/vpn-for-ai](https://venice.ai/lp/vpn-for-ai), with the subhead "The way you protect your browsing, now for everything you ask AI."
- Brand line: "Private & Unrestricted AI".
- Product posts repeat "Available anonymously" and "Nothing you send is stored".
- Positioning is anti-surveillance and anti-censorship, the same instinct that drives VPN buyers.

### Freemium funnel and pricing

| Plan | Price | Main features |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 10 text and 15 image prompts a day, base models, no sign-up to start |
| Pro | $18/mo ($216/yr) | Unlimited text, 1,000 images a day, 100 credits a month, TEE/E2EE privacy modes |
| Pro+ | $68/mo | 7,500 credits a month, credits roll over for 2 months |
| Max | $200/mo | 22,500 credits a month, 3-month rollover |
| API | Pay as you go (100 credits = $1) | Also bundled into Pro+ ($75) and Max ($225) |

- Yearly billing saves up to about 16.7%.
- No free trial found. Venice runs limited-time deals instead, for example 20% off Pro with a model launch.
- The paywall triggers on daily limits, frontier models, video and music, and the stronger privacy modes.
- Spartan Group measured the **average subscription at $23.06**: most payers take Pro.

### Retention and tokens

- **Staking:** VVV earns yield. Staked VVV can mint **DIEM**, which gives $1 a day of API credit. About 71% of VVV's market value is staked.
- **Burns:** Venice buys and burns VVV on every sale:
  - $2 per Pro, $5 per Pro+, $10 per Max subscription;
  - $5 of every $100 of credits bought (since 17 July 2026).
- **Market data (24 Oct 2026):** VVV market cap about $1.18B, fully diluted value about $1.97B, price about $24.

### Weak spots

- Brand-dependent: most search traffic is people searching its name.
- Small app-store presence, with reviews mentioning a buggy mobile app.
- Reddit users report new content filtering and the loss of email and chat support.
- Only three languages.
- Privacy outside the TEE/E2EE modes rests on Venice's word; no independent no-logs audit found.
- "Anonymous" mode still sends prompts to OpenAI, Anthropic and Google.
- No affiliate programme.
- Adult positioning creates app-store and payment-processor risk.

### Plays for PureVPN

1. **SEO:** build "private AI" and "vs" pages, plus "why AI privacy also needs a VPN" content.
2. **No-sign-up AI trial** inside the PureVPN app.
3. **Two-sided referral offer**, promoted repeatedly.
4. **Translated pages** for markets Venice skips: Hindi, Bengali and Portuguese.
5. **Answer-engine optimisation:** Venice gets real traffic from ChatGPT referrals.
6. **Counter-messaging:** "AI privacy isn't enough without network privacy". Present PureVPN as the safe, mainstream alternative to Venice's "uncensored" brand.
7. **Cheap paid search:** bid on private-AI terms Venice ignores. "uncensored ai" costs about $0.71 a click (Toolify), but only clean terms fit PureVPN.
8. **Affiliates:** recruit privacy-AI publishers and creators, since Venice has no programme.

## 2. Adult and criminal-content flags and exclusions

### What Venice markets

Venice openly markets adult NSFW text, images and roleplay "within legal limits", and runs "abliterated" models (uncensored models with refusals removed). Examples:

- "Venice wins for adults who need NSFW or erotic roleplay" (vs Character.ai page).
- 5 sitemap pages are openly adult; about 39 (about 7%) carry uncensored or adult themes.

We found **no marketing for undress/"nudify" tools, celebrity deepfakes, fraud or malware**.

### What its terms ban (ToS, 8 June 2026)

Illegal use; child abuse material including AI-generated; using a real person's likeness without consent; fraud and impersonation; malicious code.

### What has been reported

- No reported incident tying Venice to child abuse material, real-person deepfakes or nudify use.
- The 2026 investigations into nudify apps and AI child abuse material (TTP, ISD, IWF, Graphika) don't name Venice.
- Sacra flags deepfake abuse as a structural risk, not a documented case.

### Gaps to note

- The terms allow users from age 13 with parental consent, while the product is marketed as adult.
- The iOS app is rated 18+, but Google Play rates it "Everyone".
- Use is anonymous with no sign-up; we found no sign of age checks.
- Video growth came from reselling more permissive Grok Imagine access after xAI tightened its deepfake safeguards (Sacra).

### Legal context (general knowledge, to verify with counsel)

| Jurisdiction | Relevant rules |
|---|---|
| US | TAKE IT DOWN Act: 48-hour takedown of non-consensual intimate imagery, compliance due by May 2026. Sexual-deepfake bills in every state |
| UK | Online Safety Act age checks for pornography. Creating sexual deepfakes is now an offence |
| EU | AI Act Article 50 deepfake labelling, applying from August 2026 |
| Everywhere | AI-generated child abuse material is illegal |

### What to exclude from PureVPN market sizing

- NSFW, adult, porn and nude searches and use.
- Roleplay and companion "characters".
- "Uncensored", "jailbreak" and "abliterated" model demand.
- Undress, nudify, deepfake and real-person likeness tools.
- Uncensored image and video generation.

**Clean definition of private AI:** demand driven by confidentiality. That means no-logs, no-training and encrypted AI; anonymity; sensitive work, legal, health and financial data; local/on-device AI; and privacy-focused developer APIs.

**Takeaway:** the literal phrase is used mainly by tiny startups and crypto or open-source projects; Venice is the only one with scale. Norton and Bitdefender have claimed it for agents only. **No VPN brand owns "VPN for AI" for consumers.**

**Opening for PureVPN:** a three-layer "PureVPN for AI":

- network and IP privacy (the VPN);
- identity privacy (an anonymising relay);
- prompt privacy (redaction plus sealed-hardware AI).

## 4. Revenue split: developers versus consumers

### Method

Venice burns VVV at fixed published rates for each kind of sale, and each burn amount is distinct. Counting burns on the Base blockchain therefore counts sales.

### Data

- **Tokenomist (1–17 Oct 2026):**
  - 93,837 burn payments, about **$336K of sales a day**, or about **$123M a year**;
  - **81% from API credits** (about $272K/day, about $99M/yr);
  - subscriptions about $64K/day (about $23M/yr).
  - Biases: renewals may not trigger a burn, and yearly plans count as one month, both of which undercount subscriptions; payment fees aren't removed, which overcounts slightly.
- **ChainWard (14–20 Oct 2026):**
  - about 2,804 credit burns a day (about $280K/day, up 2.35x since July);
  - 3,198 new-subscription burns a day (up 2.65x since June);
  - x402 agent payments under $3K a month;
  - DIEM flat at about 28.4K staked (about $28K a day of free credit, not revenue).
- **Other signals:**
  - Spartan Group: API pricing about 20% above model cost; average subscription $23.06.
  - Sacra: API and credits are the main growth driver, with subscriptions "likely a minority".
  - Venice's OpenRouter volume grew from 40B to 76B tokens a day between August and October (Tokenomist).

### Estimate

| Segment | Revenue/yr | Share | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Credits and API | About $95–105M | About 65–81% | Medium |
| Subscriptions | About $25–50M | About 19–35% | Low–medium |
| Total | $123M (burn-counted) to over $150M (Voorhees) | | |

The credit figure includes consumers buying in-app credits for video and frontier models. **Pure developer API is an estimated 50–70% of revenue** (judgment, low–medium confidence).

**At the $123M burn-counted base** (×0.82): adult about $38M, uncensored about $19M, clean about $65M.

### Assumptions

1. **Totals** come from Voorhees's over-$150M claim and the $123M burn count, with an 81% credit share. Subscriptions sit mostly in consumer text chat and image.
2. **Developer text API is mostly coding and agents.** Almost all of Venice's 37 OpenRouter models are positioned for coding and agents; only one is "Venice Uncensored". At an assumed blended $0.3–1 per million tokens, the 76B tokens a day on OpenRouter come to about $8–28M a year.
3. **Video earns far more than its request count and leans permissive.**
   - Spartan: video "generates outsized credit revenue despite making up only a tiny fraction of requests".
   - Sacra: video generations grew 100% month on month in April and May after Grok Imagine Private launched, and revenue went from about $10M to $70M+ by July. Sacra calls Venice an "NSFW Higgsfield".
4. **Image and roleplay lean adult.**
   - Venice's comparison pages say NSFW is allowed.
   - Characters compete with Character.ai and Janitor AI.
   - Sacra names roleplay rivals (Arli AI and SillyTavern).
   - "Uncensored" searches bring about 6–12% of Venice's search traffic (Semrush estimate).
5. **The column splits are judgment.** The adult total (31%) sits at the top of our earlier independent estimate of 15–35% adult demand (30–50% "uncensored-motivated" overall).

## 6. Clean addressable figure for PureVPN

| Clean segment | Estimate | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| All clean revenue | **About $80M/yr (range $55–100M)** at the $150M base; about $65M at $123M | Venice's non-adult business |
| Clean developer and coding API | About $32M/yr | Not a market PureVPN serves today; a separate bet (private AI API or agent VPN) |
| **Clean consumer and creator use** (text chat, image, video, audio) | **About $30–50M/yr** | **The part that competes with a "PureVPN Private AI"** |

What this means:

- Venice is mostly an API and credits business.
- The clean consumer slice is modest but growing about 2.5x since mid-2026 (burn counts).
- A bundle at a Pro-like price point fits the market, given the $23.06 average subscription.

## Open gaps

- Venice publishes no revenue split by product or modality. Every matrix cell is an estimate.
- OpenRouter's per-app usage of Venice models (coding tools versus roleplay apps such as SillyTavern) wasn't available.
- Subscription renewals aren't visible on-chain, so the subscription range is wide.
- Traffic sources disagree (20M+ third-party visits versus Venice's own 1.3–1.6M).
- Not found: paying-subscriber totals, developer counts, paid ad budget, blog cadence, YouTube/influencer activity, Reddit size, and any independent no-logs audit.
- Not checked: enterprise "AI firewall" vendors in the "VPN for AI" scan; Product Hunt and app stores for that scan.
